# Instrument samples

The audio files in this folder are single-note recordings taken from the
**FluidR3_GM** General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered MP3s by the
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) project
(FluidR3_GM folder). They are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).

| Folder | Instrument (GM) | Notes kept (MIDI) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| harmonica | harmonica | 55–97, every 3rd semitone |
| bass | electric_bass_finger | 28–52, every 3rd semitone |
| piano | acoustic_grand_piano | 50–74, every 4th semitone |
| organ | drawbar_organ | 50–74, every 4th semitone |

Changes: only a subset of notes was kept; the app pitch-shifts the nearest
sample for the notes in between. The files themselves are unmodified.
