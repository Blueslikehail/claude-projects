Harp Tab Player

🎵 New lesson writes a 12-bar blues for you to learn: harmonica tab with the chords, a band to play along with, at your level and tempo.

🎧 Play along with a song takes a blues record you own, finds its key, beat and verses, tells you which harp to use and writes licks in time with it.

🎤 Live tab shows the holes you play as you play them. Transcribe a recording (beta) tries to read a solo harmonica recording — drop the file here.

Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Instrument sounds: FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen, CC BY 3.0 (credits).

Your copy of the record stays in your browser. Detection can miss on old or loose recordings — the controls above fix the key, tempo and where verses start.
