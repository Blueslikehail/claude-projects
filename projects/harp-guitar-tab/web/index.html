Harp Tab Player

🎵 New lesson writes a 12-bar blues for you to learn: harmonica tab with the chords, a band to play along with, at your level and tempo.

🎧 Play along with a song takes a blues record you own, finds its key, beat and verses, tells you which harp to use and writes licks in time with it.

🎤 Live tab shows the holes you play as you play them. Transcribe a recording (beta) tries to read a solo harmonica recording — drop the file here.

📚 Library has every lick by style and player, cards on the great harmonica players with records to hear and where to find tabs, and an importer for tab you find online or in a book — kept only in your browser.

Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

            Library

            Licks are written for this app from each style's vocabulary — "in the manner of" a player means built from that player's documented techniques, not a transcription. For the real solos, see the players' tabs and lessons, and import tab you find.

              Facts from the listed sources (searched October 2026); where sources disagree it says so. Tab and lesson links are other people's pages, found by search and not checked from here — some may have moved or be paid. "Listen" searches YouTube.

              Where to find tabs

                Paste tab from a website, a book or your notes. Most notations work: -4 or 4d or (4) for draw, ' b or " for bends, 6o for overblows, -4/5 or -4~ for shakes, -123 for chords. Lyrics and labels are skipped. What you import stays in this browser.
